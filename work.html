Tideway +214%dispatch throughput
Logistics · Platform + AI

Replacing a 14-year-old dispatch system without stopping the trucks

Tideway ran North-American freight on a dispatch tool older than most of its dispatchers — a monolith nobody dared touch. We rebuilt it as a realtime platform with an AI routing layer, migrated live, and never took the fleet offline.

340shipments re-routed daily
6.2 hrssaved per dispatcher
0hours of downtime in cutover

The approach

Strangler-fig migration, not a rewrite

We put a new event-driven core alongside the monolith and moved one route type at a time behind a feature flag. Dispatchers never saw a "big switch" — the old screens quietly stopped being used. The AI routing layer shipped last, once the data it needed was already flowing.

FERNBROOK 38 hrsreturned per analyst, weekly
Fintech · Document AI

Diligence that reads 400-page credit agreements so analysts don't have to

Fernbrook Capital's private-credit team spent nights cross-referencing covenants by hand. We built a document-intelligence pipeline that extracts, cross-checks, and drafts — with a full citation trail an investment committee will actually trust.

99.1%extraction accuracy on live deals
12×faster first-pass review
100%of outputs cite their source

The approach

Retrieval first, generation last, humans always

Every claim the system makes is grounded in a retrieved passage and linked back to the page it came from. Below a confidence threshold, the work routes to a human — and that decision is logged. The result reads less like an AI product and more like a very fast, very careful junior analyst who never guesses.

async def review_covenant(doc: Document):
    passages = await retrieve(doc, top_k=12)
    draft = await agent.run(doc, passages)

    # Nothing ships without a citation.
    assert draft.citations, "ungrounded output"

    if draft.confidence < 0.95:
        return to_human(draft, reason="low_conf")
    return finalize(draft, audit=True)
Bluepeak 4.2Mevents / min, 99.99% uptime
Energy · Cloud & Telemetry

Telemetry for a solar fleet that tripled while the bill went down

Bluepeak's distributed solar assets generated more data than their pipeline could hold. We rebuilt ingest, added anomaly detection, and gave operators tooling that surfaces a failing inverter before a customer notices — all while cutting cloud spend.

−42%cloud spend
3×data volume absorbed
11 minmean time to detect faults

The approach

Cost is a reliability feature

We moved hot telemetry to a columnar store, tiered the cold path to object storage, and right-sized the cluster with real load profiles instead of guesses. Spend dropping 42% wasn't a side effect — it was in the SLOs from day one, because an unaffordable platform is an unreliable one.
