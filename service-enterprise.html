What we build

  • Domain modeling with the people who do the work. We embed with operators before we write schemas.
  • Legacy migration without the big-bang rewrite. Strangler-fig cutovers, one workflow at a time, zero downtime.
  • Role-based access done properly. RBAC, audit logs, SSO/SCIM. Enforced in code, not policy documents.
  • Workflow engines. Approvals, escalations, and state machines that match how your org actually works.
  • Reporting your CFO signs off on. A metrics layer with one definition of every number.
  • Integration with what you already run. ERPs, CRMs, and the six systems nobody wants to touch.

The method

Migrate live, decommission by attrition

We stand the new platform up alongside the old one, move one workflow at a time behind flags, and shut the legacy system down only after weeks of it sitting unused.
