Our story

Built to leave things better.

Meridian started in 2017 with a contrarian bet: that the best software isn't built by the biggest team, but by a small one that refuses to ship anything it wouldn't want to maintain.

We'd all watched agencies win pitches with slideware and hand clients a codebase their own engineers dreaded opening. So we built the opposite. A firm where the people who scope the work are the people who write it, where every engagement ends with your team faster in the codebase than we are.

Nine years later the bet has held. We've shipped 140+ products, we still don't have a sales team, and the measure we care about most is unchanged: how well what we built runs the year after we're gone.

How we operate

Four principles we actually enforce.

Seniors only

Median 11 years of experience. We train juniors on our own products, never on yours. The person in the kickoff is the person in the PRs.

Writing is thinking

Every decision gets a document; every document gets a decider and a date. You inherit the reasoning, not just the result.

Taste is a requirement

Engineers here review Figma; designers read PRs. Craft isn't a department. It's the hiring bar for everyone.

Leave it better

We operate what we ship, then hand it over with docs, runbooks, and pairing until you don't need us. That's the whole point.
